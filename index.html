About us

Value every minute.

Caesar Apparels Limited is a professionally managed apparel manufacturer in Bangladesh, making knit and woven garments for men, women and children since 2006.

Our motto shapes how we work: efficient production, dependable execution and long-term partnerships with the brands we make for.

Our manufacturing process

Precision at every stage.

01 / 07
  1. 01

    Fabric

    Fabric sourced through long-standing partnerships with leading mills, tested for shrinkage and shade before cutting.

  2. 02

    Cutting

    CAD markers and automated cutters keep fabric use tight and panels consistent.

  3. 03

    Sewing

    Balanced lines with in-line checks at every operation, not just at the end.

  4. 04

    Washing

    Garment and denim washes developed to match your approved standard.

  5. 05

    Quality

    Measurement, workmanship and AQL audits against your specifications.

  6. 06

    Packing

    Folded, tagged and cartoned to your retail and warehouse requirements.

  7. 07

    Shipment

    Booked out of Chittagong port with full documentation and on-time tracking.
